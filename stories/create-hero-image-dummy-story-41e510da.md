## Introduction to Environmental Earth Observation
Satellite remote sensing provides critical continuous measurements across continental ecosystems, tracking subtle climate shifts, vegetation canopy health, and water quality dynamics. Multi-sensor missions such as Copernicus Sentinel-2, Sentinel-3, and international partner instruments offer multi-spectral and thermal observations that uncover changes in surface vegetation and inland aquatic habitats.

### Official Dashboard Narratives

## Summary & Data Sources
Long-term monitoring through satellite constellations offers an objective baseline to analyze climate pressures on inland waters and terrestrial vegetation. Open access data cubes and automated semantic classification allow researchers and environmental managers to respond proactively to rapid ecosystem transitions.
