## Atlantic Ocean Dynamics and Marine Ecosystems off Portugal
The Atlantic coast of mainland Portugal and its adjacent Exclusive Economic Zone (EEZ) form part of the Canary Current Upwelling System—one of the world's major eastern boundary upwelling ecosystems. Seasonal northerly winds driving Ekman transport cause cold, nutrient-laden subsurface waters to rise toward the sunlit photic zone, fueling explosive blooms of phytoplankton that sustain sardine, mackerel, and pelagic fisheries along the continental shelf.

#### 5. Cape St. Vincent & Algarve Upwelling Center
At Cabo de São Vicente (Sagres), prevailing currents round the southwestern corner of the European continent. This persistent upwelling center transports nutrient-rich filaments far offshore into the Gulf of Cádiz ([ESA Ocean Science](https://www.esa.int)).

## Synthesis & Marine Resource Management
Long-term monitoring of the Portuguese marine realm demonstrates how atmospheric forcing, seabed bathymetry (such as the Nazaré and Cascais canyons), and coastal upwelling interact to sustain biological productivity. As sea surface temperatures rise under global climate change, tracking changes in phytoplankton bloom timing, thermal stratification, and nutrient availability becomes essential for preserving pelagic biodiversity and guiding sustainable maritime management along the Iberian Atlantic.
