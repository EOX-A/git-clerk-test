## Observing Marine Ecosystems and Primary Productivity
### Understanding Climate Change Effects on Marine Resources

Changes in sea surface temperature, ocean currents, and biogeochemical cycles directly influence primary productivity in marine ecosystems. Microscopic organisms—primarily phytoplankton—form the base of the marine food web, driving global carbon fixation and supporting commercial fisheries.

#### Official Dashboard Narratives
Official Earth observation narratives document the response of marine ecosystems to fluctuating oceanographic conditions:
* [Understanding climate change effects on water temperature and ocean currents is vital to managing these resources](https://ESA-eodashboards.github.io/eodashboard-narratives/ocean-colours.md) — an official trilateral narrative by [ESA](https://www.esa.int), [NASA](https://www.nasa.gov/), and [JAXA](https://global.jaxa.jp/) featured on the [EO Dashboard](https://eodashboard.org/).
