## Introduction to Environmental Monitoring
Earth observation satellites provide an unprecedented perspective on terrestrial ecosystems, urban expansion, and climatic variability across our planet. By combining multi-spectral optical instruments and radar sensors, researchers and decision-makers monitor landscape transitions with high temporal frequency and spatial precision.
